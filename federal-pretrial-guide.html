Technical Command Roadmap

The 4 Pre-Trial Milestones in Federal Court

We guide families and coordinate with defense counsel to build a technical release package for the magistrate judge.

Step 1 · Hours 0–6

Emergency Inmate Locator

Immediate tracking across USMS, MCC San Diego, and Otay Mesa Detention Center. Setting up emergency family communications and medical notifications.

Step 2 · Hours 6–24

PSO Interview Prep

Coaching the defendant or family for the Pretrial Services Officer (PSO) interview—the single most critical factor determining detention recommendations.

Step 3 · Hours 24–72

§ 3142 Bail Hearing Package

Compiling physical Tie-to-Community Binders (sureties, housing verification, employment proof, GPS monitoring consent) for defense counsel to present in court.

Step 4 · Months 1–3

Discovery Indexing

Technical indexing of electronic discovery (CBP secondary inspection footage, GPS tracker logs) to support defense counsel in building a no-knowledge defense.
